Program checks

Reads Fanuc-style milling code: G0–G3 (IJK or R, G17/18/19, helical), G90/G91, G20/G21, G28, G43/H, drill cycles G73/G81–G89 with G98/G99, A/B/C rotary moves, G68 / G68.2 tilted planes and G43.4 TCP. Tool comp (G41/G42) is shown on the programmed path, not the offset path. Macros (#, IF, WHILE) and subprograms are skipped and flagged. This is a programming check, not a material-removal simulation: it does not know your stock, fixtures or holder lengths.
